Episode Production

Episode Production is Joel Bertin's personal desktop workflow for rendering, reviewing and assembling video episodes.

Its Google Drive connection uploads temporary review videos to a designated private folder, verifies those uploads, and removes the exact cloud copies after the owner explicitly approves them. Local production files are preserved.

Pushover sends the owner status updates and private review links. Separate approval steps cover individual scene assets and the assembled episode.

This website provides information about the workflow. It does not host review videos, collect Google credentials, or provide a public sign-in service.
